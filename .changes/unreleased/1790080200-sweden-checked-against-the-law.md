---
type: Changed
title: Sweden's rules checked against the sources
---

A credit note now prints the number of the invoice it credits; an invoice can be marked reverse charge or exempt, with the buyer's VAT number required and the page saying so; an invoice in another currency asks for the VAT in kronor; a limited company must print its seat; and the page carries the payment terms, the Interest Act's rate and the optional 450 kr late fee and 60 kr reminder fee.
