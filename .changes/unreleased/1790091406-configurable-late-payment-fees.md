title: Late-payment figures you can set, where the law lets you

The company's late-payment lines now say where each figure comes from: the interest rate is yours to set when one is agreed and falls back to the Interest Act's when left empty, the reminder fee takes any amount up to the statutory 60 kr, and the 450 kr late fee stays a plain switch because räntelagen fixes that amount.
