# Company

Who the invoices are from. One form: the **region** whose rules apply (Sweden
today), the party form with the region's seller identifiers (organisation
number, VAT number, registered office), the **payment details** (bankgiro,
plusgiro, IBAN, BIC, an OCR reference, the F-tax flag, and the late-payment
lines — an interest rate, which is empty until a rate is agreed and then
replaces the Interest Act's own on the page; the 450 kr late fee a business
customer owes, a switch with no amount because räntelagen 4 a § fixes the
figure; and the reminder fee, a switch with an amount beneath it, capped at
the statutory 60 kr. All three may only be charged when the invoice says so,
and each field says under it where its figure comes from), the
**company form** (a limited company must print its seat; nobody else must),
and the **first invoice number** the series starts at. The notices the region will print on
every invoice are listed at the bottom.

Saved with a button rather than on every keystroke, because every save that
changed something is a revision; the **History** button opens them with
restore, the way a customer's does. The same details are editable on a draft
invoice's page, where the payment block and the "From" block are the company.
