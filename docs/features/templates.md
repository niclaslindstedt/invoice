# Templates

What a new invoice starts from. A template holds a name, the days until due,
the default VAT rate, unit and currency, the default note, whether the total is
rounded, the **sections** a new page starts with and their order, and the
**look** — one of five typefaces (sans, serif, display, geometric, mono), one
of seven accents, white or cream paper.

One template is the **default**: **New invoice** seeds from it, and a draft
can have any template applied from its details card. Every invoice keeps its
own copy of the layout, so editing a template never rearranges an invoice
already written.
