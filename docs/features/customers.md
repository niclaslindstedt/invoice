# Customers

One card per customer: the name, the address, the customer number, the
contact person, and how many invoices it has. Archived customers sort last
and are left out of **New invoice**.

The editor is the party form — name, street, postcode, city, country, contact
person, email, phone, website — plus the region's identifiers for a buyer (an
organisation number in Sweden, checked as you type but never refused), a
customer number of your own, the **default price per unit** a dropped-in hour
starts at, notes, and archive.

## History

Every save that changed something appends a version. The clock glyph — on the
card and in the editor — opens the history: every version, newest first, with
what changed from the one before, and the version in full when picked.
**Restore this version** makes it the current one, as a new version, so the
history goes on being the whole story.

The same edits arrive from the invoice editor: correcting a customer's
address on a draft is the same save, and the same revision.
